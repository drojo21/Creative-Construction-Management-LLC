# Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization Checklist
**Creative Construction Management LLC · ROC 355392 · Tucson, AZ**

A fully optimized GBP is the single highest-ROI marketing move for a local contractor. It's free, it shows up in Google Maps results, and it's where 80% of "[trade] near me" searches get answered before the first organic link.

This is an 8-step checklist. Work through it top to bottom. Total time: about 30 minutes for the profile + ongoing for posts/reviews.

---

## Step 1 · Claim or create your profile (5 min)

1. Go to https://business.google.com and sign in with the same Google account you use for `ccmanagementaz@gmail.com`.
2. In the top search bar, type: **Creative Construction Management** (Tucson). If a listing already exists (Google sometimes auto-generates them from public data), click "Claim this business". If not, click "Add business" and create one.
3. Use this **exact** business name: `Creative Construction Management LLC` — do **not** stuff keywords into the name ("Creative Construction Management LLC - Tucson Remodeling Contractor" is a Google Guidelines violation that will get the listing suspended).
4. Choose verification method. Postcard to physical address is standard; some contractor accounts qualify for video verification (you walk the phone around your truck/tools/job site).

---

## Step 2 · Complete business information (10 min)

Fill in every field. Empty fields rank lower.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | `Creative Construction Management LLC` |
| Primary category | **General Contractor** |
| Additional categories | Construction Company · Remodeler · Home Builder · Commercial Contractor |
| Phone | `(520) 273-9295` |
| Short name / URL | `creativeconstructionaz` (for `g.page/creativeconstructionaz`) |
| Business hours | Mon–Fri 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM · Sat 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM · Sun closed |
| Special hours | Update around major holidays (Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas) |
| Website | Your deployed URL (e.g. `https://creativeconstructionmanagementaz.com`) |
| Appointment URL | Same — or specifically the `#contact` section |
| Service area | Tucson AZ · Marana AZ · Oro Valley AZ · Vail AZ (set as **service-area business**, not storefront, unless customers visit an office) |
| Attributes | "LGBTQ+ friendly", "Women-owned" if applicable, "Veteran-owned" if applicable, "Online estimates: Yes", "Onsite services: Yes" |
| Opening date | Your actual business start month/year |

**Important:** when you choose "service-area business" Google hides your street address from the public listing — this is fine for contractors and actually preferred. Customers come to them, not the other way around.

## Step 3 · Write the business description (5 min)

GBP allows 750 characters. Keywords matter — but so does readability. Use this:

> Creative Construction Management LLC is a licensed general contractor serving Tucson, Marana, Oro Valley, and Vail. We hold an Arizona KB-1 Dual Building Contractor license (ROC 355392), one of the broadest general-contracting classes — covering both residential and commercial work under one qualification.
>
> Our focus: home additions, kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole-home renovations, commercial tenant improvements, and full-service general contracting project management. Every project starts with a walkthrough, a written line-item estimate, and a clear schedule.
>
> Owner Jesus Manuel Lopez is on every job. Call (520) 273-9295 for a free estimate.

Character count: ~720 — safely under the 750 cap.

Keywords naturally included: `Tucson general contractor`, `licensed contractor`, `Arizona KB-1`, `home additions`, `kitchen remodel`, `bathroom remodel`, `commercial tenant improvements`, `ROC 355392`, and all four service-area cities.

## Step 5 · Upload photos (15 min — the single biggest ranking lift)

GBP listings with **10+ photos** get roughly 2× the clicks of listings with just a logo. Shoot for:

| Category | Minimum | What to upload |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | 1 | Square logo on transparent or white background (500×500 min) |
| Cover photo | 1 | Landscape hero shot — a finished kitchen, addition exterior, or crisp commercial build-out (1200×628) |
| Team | 1–2 | Jesus and any crew at a job site — faces, smiling, real work context |
| At work | 4–6 | Framing, finishing, clean-up — action shots mid-project |
| Completed projects | 6–10 | Before/after pairs are gold. Tag the city in the caption |
| Equipment / truck | 1–2 | Truck with logo, tools laid out clean, permit posted on site |

**Photo naming convention** (Google reads filenames — name them descriptively):
- `creative-construction-kitchen-remodel-tucson.jpg`
- `creative-construction-bathroom-renovation-marana.jpg`
- `creative-construction-room-addition-oro-valley.jpg`

**Ongoing:** upload 2–3 new photos per month. Google rewards active profiles.

## Weekly ritual (10 min every Monday)

- [ ] Check for new GBP messages — respond within 4 hours during weekdays
- [ ] Reply to any new reviews (yes, even 5-star ones — Google rewards response rate)
- [ ] Post one Google Post (rotate: before/after · FAQ · seasonal update · tip)
- [ ] Upload 2–3 new job site / project photos
- [ ] Text the review link to any customer whose job wrapped in the past week

---

## Monthly ritual (30 min, first of the month)

- [ ] Check GBP Insights → note search terms, call count, direction requests, photo views
- [ ] Update services list if any offerings changed
- [ ] Re-check hours for any upcoming holidays
- [ ] Compare review count to the prior month — target: **2+ new reviews per month minimum**

---

## Success benchmarks

| Timeframe | Review count target | Monthly profile views | Monthly calls from GBP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | 3–5 reviews | 200–500 | 5–15 |
| Month 3 | 10–15 reviews | 800–1,500 | 20–40 |
| Month 6 | 25+ reviews | 2,000+ | 50+ |
| Month 12 | 50+ reviews · 4.8★ avg | 4,000+ | 100+ |

At month 12, a well-maintained Tucson general contractor GBP typically drives 3–5 booked jobs per month on its own.
