## Post 3 · Educational Tip
**Post type:** Authority-building · give-before-you-ask content
**Suggested time:** Monday, 8:30 AM

> Quick tip for Tucson homeowners thinking about a remodel:
>
> Get three bids — not two, not one. And look at how they're written more than the number at the bottom.
>
> A real estimate should have:
> · Line items (demo, rough plumbing, framing, drywall, tile, finish carpentry — each separate)
> · A materials allowance (e.g. "$8,000 for tile" — so if you want fancier tile, you know what it adds)
> · A written schedule with milestones
> · Payment schedule tied to milestones, not calendar dates
>
> If a bid is a single number on a napkin, that's not an estimate — that's a guess. Licensed contractors in Arizona (like us — ROC 355392) are required by law to provide written contracts before starting work. Don't skip that step.

**Photo direction:** Phone photo of a real line-item estimate spread on a clipboard (cover dollar amounts with tape for privacy). Or a clean infographic with the four bullet points.

**Hashtags:** #TucsonHomeowner #RemodelTips #LicensedContractor #ConstructionTips #TucsonContractor #AZROC #HomeRenovation #SmartSpending
