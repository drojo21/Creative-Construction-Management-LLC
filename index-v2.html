Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have one that isn’t answered here? Call (520) 273-9295 — you’ll talk to someone who actually works on the jobs.

Is Creative Construction Management a licensed contractor in Arizona?

Yes. Creative Construction Management LLC is a fully licensed Arizona contractor holding a KB-1 Dual Building Contractor license, ROC 355392, in active status. The license covers both residential and commercial general construction and can be verified on the Arizona Registrar of Contractors website at roc.az.gov.

What areas around Tucson do you serve?

We serve Tucson and the surrounding communities including Marana, Oro Valley, and Vail. For projects outside these areas in Southern Arizona, call (520) 273-9295 to discuss feasibility.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Free, no-obligation estimates are available for home additions, remodels, and commercial projects in our service area. Request one using the quote form on this site or call (520) 273-9295. We typically respond within one business day.

What does your project process look like?

Every project follows a clear four-step process: (1) consultation and on-site assessment, (2) detailed written estimate and project plan, (3) permits, scheduling, and construction with regular progress updates, (4) final walkthrough, inspection, and warranty handoff. Most residential remodels begin within two to four weeks of contract signing, depending on scope and permit timing.

Does your KB-1 license cover both residential and commercial work?

Yes. A KB-1 is a Dual Building Contractor license, which means we are legally permitted to perform both residential (homes, additions, remodels) and commercial (retail, office, light industrial) general construction in the state of Arizona. This is one of the broadest general-contracting license classes issued by the Arizona ROC.

How long do home additions and remodels typically take?

Timeline depends on scope. A bathroom remodel typically runs three to six weeks. A kitchen remodel runs six to ten weeks. Room additions commonly take three to six months from permit approval to final inspection. We provide a realistic written schedule with every estimate so you know what to expect.
