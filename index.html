What We Deliver

Seven Core Deliverables

Every engagement includes these structured deliverables so your team has everything needed to execute with confidence.

01

Pre-Construction Schedule

A detailed baseline project schedule with milestones, critical path activities, and resource allocation — delivered before the first shovel hits the ground. Includes Gantt-formatted timeline and contractor task assignments.

  • Critical path method (CPM) schedule
  • Milestone tracking chart
  • Look-ahead schedule (3-week rolling)
02

Budget & Cost Control Report

A line-item budget breakdown with committed costs, actual-to-date tracking, and variance analysis. Updated monthly — or at any project gate — so contractors always know where the money stands.

  • Line-item cost breakdown (CSI format)
  • Monthly variance report
  • Contingency & allowance tracking
03

Scope of Work Document

A clearly defined, division-by-division scope of work that eliminates ambiguity between owner, contractor, and subs. Reduces change orders caused by interpretation gaps before construction begins.

  • Division-based scope narrative
  • Inclusions & exclusions matrix
  • Interface responsibility chart (IRC)
04

Permit & Code Compliance Package

All permit applications, inspection checklists, and code-compliance documentation organized for submission to the AHJ. We coordinate with Tucson & Pima County offices so contractors aren't chasing paperwork.

  • Permit application preparation
  • AHJ inspection checklist
  • IBC / IRC / NEC reference log
05

Subcontractor Coordination Plan

A structured coordination package that defines sub-tier responsibilities, sequencing, and communication protocols — reducing conflicts on the job site and keeping every trade moving in the right order.

  • Trade sequencing matrix
  • Subcontractor contact & submittal log
  • Weekly coordination meeting agenda
06

Quality Control & Inspection Log

Field-ready inspection forms and a running QC log that documents work-in-place against approved submittals. Supports ROC compliance and provides a defensible quality record throughout construction.

  • Phase-by-phase inspection forms
  • Deficiency tracking & resolution log
  • Photo documentation protocol
07

Project Closeout & Punch List

A comprehensive closeout package that gets the project to substantial completion and final sign-off — including punch list management, warranty documentation, lien waiver coordination, and as-built record assembly.

  • Punch list creation & tracking
  • Warranty & O&M manual compilation
  • Final lien waiver & closeout checklist
