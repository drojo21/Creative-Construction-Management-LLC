How It Works

Five Steps, No Surprises

The same process on a bathroom remodel as on a ground-up commercial build — so you always know what happens next and who is responsible for it.

  1. 1

    Call & scope

    A real conversation about what you want, what it needs to cost, and whether we're the right contractor for it. Same-day or next-business-day response.

  2. 2

    Walkthrough & written estimate

    We walk the site, measure, and send a line-item estimate with a schedule and a payment milestone plan. Free, and yours to compare against other bids.

  3. 3

    Plans & permits

    We handle drawings, submittals, and plan review with the City of Tucson or Pima County, and we schedule every inspection. You don't stand in line.

  4. 4

    Build

    One point of contact, weekly progress updates, and a clean site. Any change to scope gets priced and approved in writing before it happens.

  5. 5

    Walkthrough & warranty

    We walk the finished work with you, close out the punch list, and hand over warranty and closeout documents before the final payment.

Built on Craft. Run on Communication.

Creative Construction Management LLC is a Tucson-based general contracting firm led by Jesus Manuel Lopez. We focus on a short list of things done well: residential additions and remodels for homeowners who want their space to finally fit their life, and commercial work for operators who need a builder they can actually reach on the phone.

Every project we take on starts with a real consultation, a written scope, and a clear schedule — so there are no surprises on day one, day thirty, or at final walkthrough.
