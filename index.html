About Us

A Licensed Tucson Builder You Can Talk To

Built on Craft. Run on Communication.

Creative Construction Management LLC is a Tucson-based general contracting firm led by Jesus Manuel Lopez. We focus on a short list of things done well: residential additions and remodels for homeowners who want their space to finally fit their life, and commercial work for operators who need a builder they can actually reach on the phone.

Every project we take on starts with a real consultation, a written scope, and a clear schedule — so there are no surprises on day one, day thirty, or at final walkthrough.

  • Licensed dual-class contractor

    KB-1 is one of Arizona's broadest general-contracting classes — we're legally qualified for both residential and commercial work.

  • Clear, written scopes and estimates

    You get line-item pricing, a schedule, and a single point of contact — not a napkin sketch.

  • Owner-involved on every job

    The owner walks the site, not just the estimator. Decisions happen fast because the person making them is already there.

  • Local to Tucson — serving the places we live

    Based in Tucson 85746, serving Marana, Oro Valley, and Vail. Local vendors, local inspectors, local references.
